Three steps, then it stays out of your way.

Set your targets

Enter your height, weight, goal and activity level once. Cutline works out a daily calorie target with the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, plus protein and net-carb targets for keto or low carb.

Log as you eat

Type a food and how much you had. You see calories, protein, net carbs and fat, and what’s left for the day, before you press Enter.

Watch the trend

Weigh in each morning and Cutline charts your trend. After 14 logged days it checks your targets against your real results and offers a better maintenance number.

Built from a real grocery list.

Generic foods use USDA FoodData Central values per 100 grams, about 7,400 of them. Brand items use the label for one serving, so typing 1 logs a whole cup, bottle or bar. Restaurant items use each chain’s published numbers and log by count, so 2 means two. Anything missing takes a minute to add from its label.

Private by default.

Cutline is one web page with no ads and no analytics. There is no Cutline account to create and no Cutline server holding your diary.

  • On the web, your log stays in this browser on this device.
  • Opened inside Claude, it saves to your own Claude account instead.
  • The code is open, so you can read exactly what it does. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Questions

How accurate are the numbers?

Close enough to steer by. Generic foods are USDA averages from FoodData Central. Weights are for the food as the name describes it, raw or cooked. Brand items match the package label, but recipes change, so check yours.

What if my food isn’t in the list?

Open Custom foods in the tracker. On the web version you can scan or type the barcode to look it up in Open Food Facts; inside Claude you can photograph the nutrition label. Check the numbers, save, and it’s searchable from then on.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It’s built for phones first. On the web version, add the page to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Will I lose my log if I clear my browser?

On the web, yes, because the log lives in your browser. Opened inside Claude, it’s kept in your Claude account.

Is this medical advice?

No. Targets come from standard formulas and are estimates. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before a big change to how you eat.
